Who We Are

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Founded over two decades ago, Light High School has grown into one of Uganda's most respected secondary institutions. We offer both O-Level (UCE) and A-Level (UACE) programmes, guiding students through their formative years with quality education, moral grounding, and practical life skills.

Our dedicated staff, modern facilities, and vibrant school community create an environment where every student can discover their potential and thrive.

What We Offer

Academic Programmes

Structured learning pathways designed to develop critical thinking, creativity, and academic mastery at every level.

O-Level (UCE)

Four years of comprehensive learning covering sciences, humanities, and languages, culminating in the Uganda Certificate of Education exams.

Learn more
A-Level (UACE)

Two-year advanced programme with Science, Arts, and Business combinations, preparing students for the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education and university entry.

Learn more
Science & Labs

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories where students engage in hands-on experiments and develop scientific thinking.

Learn more
ICT & Computing

A modern computer lab with internet access, teaching practical digital literacy and computer science skills essential for the 21st century.

Learn more
Arts & Humanities

Literature, History, Geography, and Fine Art programmes that cultivate creative expression and an appreciation for culture and society.

Learn more
Co-Curricular

Sports, music, drama, debate, and numerous clubs that develop leadership, teamwork, and confidence beyond the classroom.

Learn more
