Life at Light HS

More Than Just Classrooms

At Light High School we believe that true education goes far beyond textbooks. Our vibrant school community provides every student with opportunities to discover their passions, build character, develop leadership skills, and forge lasting friendships in a safe and nurturing environment.

From award-winning drama groups and competitive sports teams to community service initiatives and a strong student council — life at Light HS is full, purposeful, and fun.

Residential Life

Boarding at Light HS

A Safe, Structured Home Away from Home

Our boarding programme accommodates 600+ students in separate, supervised boys' and girls' dormitories. Each house has a resident house parent and a nursing staff member on call.

Wellbeing

Pastoral Care & Support

We are committed to the holistic wellbeing of every student. Our pastoral support system ensures that no learner feels alone — whether dealing with academic pressure, personal challenges, or adjustment difficulties.

Form Teacher System

Each class has a dedicated form teacher who meets with students weekly and tracks individual progress.

Guidance & Counselling

A full-time school counsellor is available for individual or group sessions throughout the week.

Peer Support Programme

Senior students trained as peer supporters help younger students navigate transitions and challenges.

Parent Communication

Regular parent–teacher meetings, SMS updates, and an open-door policy for parental concerns.
