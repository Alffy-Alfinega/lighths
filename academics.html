Academic Programmes

Rigorous, well-rounded programmes designed to unlock each student's potential and prepare them for university and beyond.

Senior 1 – Senior 4

O-Level Programme (UCE)

Our four-year Ordinary Level programme leads students to the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) examination set by UNEB. Students study a broad mix of compulsory and elective subjects, building a strong academic foundation across Sciences, Humanities, Languages, and Mathematics.

Small class sizes ensure every student receives individual attention from our qualified and experienced teaching staff. Regular mock exams and internal assessments track progress and identify areas for improvement throughout the course.

Senior 5 – Senior 6

A-Level Programme (UACE)

The two-year Advanced Level programme prepares students for the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) and university entrance. Students choose from Science, Arts, or Business subject combinations tailored to their career goals.

Our A-Level students benefit from dedicated career counselling, university application guidance, and close mentorship to ensure they secure places at Uganda's top universities and international institutions.

Departments

Our Academic Departments

Each department is staffed by experienced, UNEB-trained educators dedicated to student success.

Sciences Department

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Combined Science. Three fully equipped labs support hands-on practical learning throughout the year.

Mathematics Department

Pure Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, and Additional Mathematics for high-achieving students targeting science and engineering programmes.

Languages Department

English Language, English Literature, and Luganda. Strong oral and written communication skills are core to our curriculum.

Humanities Department

History, Geography, Religious Education, and Divinity. Students explore Uganda's heritage and the wider world through rich, contextual study.

Business & Commerce

Entrepreneurship, Economics, and Accounting prepare students for the growing demands of Uganda's business and financial sectors.

ICT Department

Computer Science, Information Technology, and Digital Literacy in a modern 50-PC lab with high-speed internet access.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular Activities

We believe education extends beyond textbooks. Our vibrant co-curricular programme develops leadership, creativity, and confidence.
