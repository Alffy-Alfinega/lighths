Find Us

Our Location

We are located along Kampala–Entebbe Road, easily accessible by public and private transport from Kampala city centre (~25 min) and Entebbe (~20 min).

Getting Here

From Kampala: Take the Entebbe Road southbound. The school is on the right side after the Kajjansi trading centre, opposite the Total petrol station. Matatus (taxis) from Kampala Old Park heading to Entebbe stop directly at the gate.
