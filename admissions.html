Admissions

Join the Light High School family. Applications for the 2025/2026 academic year are now open for S.1 and S.5 entrants.

Applications Close: 30th November 2025 for S.1  |  Reporting Date: 5th February 2026  |  Apply online below

Entry Requirements

Who Can Apply?

Senior One (S.1) Entry

For students completing Primary Seven (PLE).

  • Uganda Primary Leaving Examination (PLE) results slip
  • Minimum aggregate of 20 or below (Distinction or Credit)
  • Birth certificate or baptism card (original & copy)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Completed application form (available below)
  • Non-refundable application fee of UGX 20,000
Senior Five (S.5) Entry

For students who have completed O-Level (UCE).

  • UCE results slip showing Division I or II
  • Minimum of 6 passes including relevant A-Level subjects
  • Official school leaving certificate
  • Birth certificate (original & copy)
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Non-refundable application fee of UGX 20,000
How to Apply

Application Process

Follow these simple steps to secure your place at Light High School.

1
Obtain the Application Form

Fill in the online form below, or visit the school office to collect a physical form. A non-refundable fee of UGX 20,000 applies.

2
Gather Required Documents

Compile all supporting documents listed in the entry requirements above. Ensure all originals and certified copies are ready.

3
Submit Your Application

Submit the completed form and documents in person at the school office, or via email to admissions@lighths.ac.ug by the deadline.

4
Admission Interview & Assessment

Shortlisted applicants will be contacted for a brief interview. S.1 entrants sit a short placement assessment in English and Mathematics.

5
Receive Admission Letter

Successful candidates receive an official admission letter detailing reporting instructions, fees, and the required items list.

6
Report & Join Us!

Pay the required fees, collect your uniform from the school store, and report on the designated date. Welcome to the Light family!
