About Light High School

Over two decades of nurturing bright minds and building exceptional character in the heart of Uganda.

Our Story

A School Born from a Vision

Light High School was founded in 1999 by a group of passionate educators who believed that every Ugandan child deserved access to quality secondary education. Starting with just three streams and 180 students in a rented building, the school quickly earned a reputation for academic rigour and moral excellence.

By 2005, the school had constructed its own permanent premises on Plot 42, Kampala–Entebbe Road, adding science laboratories, a library, and dormitories to accommodate the growing demand for boarding places. Today, we serve over 1,200 students from across Uganda and the wider East African region.

Registered with the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB) and the Ministry of Education and Sports, Light High School continues to grow — investing in facilities, teacher training, and student welfare to remain at the forefront of secondary education.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To provide quality, holistic education that equips students with the knowledge, skills, and moral values necessary to serve God, Uganda, and the global community with integrity and excellence.

Our Vision

To be a leading and recognised centre of academic excellence in Uganda, producing ethical, innovative, and globally competitive graduates who positively transform their communities.

Integrity

Excellence

Striving for the highest standards in learning.

Community

Building belonging and mutual respect.

Innovation

Embracing new ideas and critical thinking.

Discipline

Self-control and respect for order.

Service

Contributing meaningfully to Uganda & beyond.
